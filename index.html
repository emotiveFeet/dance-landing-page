Meet Aishwarya

Dance with emotion.

I'm Aishwarya Vardhan, a dancer, choreographer and dance teacher based in Bangalore.


With five years of Kathak training and a love for contemporary, semi-classical, Bollywood, freestyle and hip-hop, I explore movement across different styles while keeping expression at the heart of dance.


Through Emotive Feet, I create spaces where people can learn, move, perform and most importantly — enjoy dancing.
