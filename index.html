About

Dance is more than steps.

I'm Aarav, a choreographer and dance educator based in Bengaluru. I teach commercial choreography, freestyle and performance-focused movement while helping dancers build confidence, musicality and their own style.


From beginner-friendly classes to stage performances and workshops, my goal is simple — make people move, create and enjoy the process.
